# How-to-be-an-astronaut----NASASPACEAPP
Nasaspaceapp challenge. La punta,  San Luis 2026.

NASA SPACE APPS CHALLENGE (2026)
"Build a Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer"
LOS SUPERHIJITUS

Our project is a web platform designed to simulate space missions divided into levels. At each stage, the player faces different scenarios where they must make decisions regarding resources, survival, and logistics that will affect—positively or negatively—their Energy, Health, and Shield attributes.

In addition to featuring an attractive and accessible interface, the platform integrates real information and data about what an astronaut experiences in space. This gives the game a scientific foundation and allows it to recreate the true conditions of the space environment realistically.

The main goal is to transform complex concepts of space exploration into an educational and fun experience for younger audiences.
